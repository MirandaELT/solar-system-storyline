Project Overview

This project showcases a self-guided, interactive eLearning module developed in Articulate Storyline 360. It transforms text-heavy source material about the Solar System into an engaging digital learning experience through interactive content, learner-controlled navigation and assessment activities.

The project was developed using a 14-day Storyline trial to demonstrate instructional design, eLearning development, accessibility and evaluation skills.

Context & Audience

The module was designed for adults exploring the Solar System, including those preparing to teach the topic. Learners may have varying levels of prior subject knowledge and digital confidence, and some may speak English as an additional language.

The design prioritises clear language, manageable sections, visual explanations and opportunities for learners to explore content at their own pace.

Design Approach

The module covers five topics: The Sun, The Eight Planets, Dwarf Planets, Moons, and Comets and Asteroids.

Learners begin with an introduction and learning outcomes before accessing a clickable topic menu. They can follow the recommended sequence or select individual topics.

The learning experience includes labelled graphics, tabs, interactive layers, flip cards, optional information markers and a five-question final assessment.

Accessibility considerations include keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, alternative text, focus order and assessment interaction choices.

Iterative development, testing and evaluation informed refinements in response to technical constraints, accessibility requirements and feedback.

Tools & Delivery

Articulate Storyline 360 was used for development, Windows Narrator for screen reader testing, ChatGPT for design exploration and troubleshooting, and Netlify for hosting.

The module was developed as a portfolio project rather than implemented with a live learner cohort.

Evaluation & Next Steps

The development and evaluation documentation records testing, design refinements and outstanding quality assurance issues identified after publication.

Future improvements would include resolving outstanding assessment feedback, navigation and hyperlink issues, completing further accessibility and functionality testing, and republishing the module.

Additional formative checks and a glossary of astronomical terminology would provide further learner support.
